Learn to spot phishing attempts

Phishing messages can look like they come from colleagues, familiar companies, or services you use. Before acting on an email, use these checks:

  • Check who sent it. Look closely at the full sender address and watch for misspellings or domains that do not match the organization.
  • Be cautious with unexpected links. Hover over a link to inspect its destination before opening it. When in doubt, go to the service's known website directly.
  • Pause on urgent or unusual requests. Messages pressuring you to act quickly, share information, or bypass normal steps deserve extra scrutiny.
  • Treat unexpected attachments carefully. Do not open files you were not expecting, even if the message appears familiar.
  • Verify requests another way. Contact the person or organization using a trusted phone number or channel, not the details in the message.
  • Report suspicious messages. Use your organization's approved reporting process so the security team can investigate.
